FAQ

Questions artists ask

Do I need to set anything up?

No. Send me your Instagram and price list — I build your app with your services, photos, hours and rules, and send you the link. You check it and share it with your clients. Later changes take a minute in your dashboard.

I already use Booksy or GlossGenius. Does it work with that?

Yes. Keep your booking system: the Book button in your app can open your Booksy, GlossGenius or Vagaro page, while your app gives clients your prices, looks, aftercare and answers. Switch to the built-in booking whenever you’re ready.

How do clients install it?

They open your link — satinbook.com/your-name — and tap Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or Install on Android. Your icon and name land on their Home Screen. No App Store, nothing to download.

What if I cancel?

Your app shows clients a short “taking a break” page, and you can come back any time with everything in place. No contracts, no fees. Want your client list? Ask and I’ll send it to you.
