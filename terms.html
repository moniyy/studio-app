Satinbook gives independent beauty artists their own booking app. These terms apply to artists who use Satinbook and to clients who book through an artist’s app. By using Satinbook you agree to them — they’re short, please read them.

1. What Satinbook is

Satinbook hosts a booking app for each studio: services and prices, free times, booking, reminders and a dashboard for the artist. Satinbook is a tool — we are not a beauty provider and not a party to your appointment.

2. Bookings are between the client and the studio

  • Each studio sets its own services, prices, hours and policies — cancellations, late fees, deposits, no-shows. When you book, you agree to that studio’s policy, shown before you confirm.
  • Questions about an appointment, its price or quality go to the studio. Satinbook can’t change or refund a studio’s decision.
  • Please give correct contact details and cancel through your booking link if your plans change.

3. Payments and deposits

Satinbook does not accept, hold or process payments. Deposits and payments go directly from the client to the artist through Cash App, Zelle, Venmo or another app the artist chooses. Any refund or dispute is between the client, the artist and that payment app. Satinbook only records what the artist marks — for example “deposit received”.

4. For artists

  • Plan. The first 30 days are free. After that the plan is $29 per month (or the price agreed with you, such as the founding price). You can cancel any time; the current month isn’t refunded, and nothing more is charged.
  • Your account. Keep your password private and tell us if you think someone else is using your account. You’re responsible for what happens in your studio’s dashboard, including what your staff do there.
  • Your content. Your photos, texts and prices stay yours. You allow Satinbook to host and show them in your app and to make the app work. Only upload what you have the right to use.
  • Your clients. Use clients’ details only to serve their appointments and to stay in touch about your studio, and follow the laws that apply to you, including on messages and privacy.
  • Pausing. If a plan isn’t paid or these terms are broken, we may pause the studio’s app. Clients then see a short-break page. On request we’ll send you your client list.
